# Planto 🌱

Planto is a plant care reminder app for iPhone. It keeps all your plants in one place and reminds you when it's time to water them.

## Main Features

- **Add a plant reminder** with its name, room, light, watering days, and water amount
- **Edit** a plant's details by tapping on it
- **Delete** a plant by swiping left, or from the edit screen
- **Check off** plants after watering them
- **Track your progress** with an animated progress bar
- **Get a celebration screen** when all plants are watered 🎉
- **Receive local notifications** to remind you to water your plants
- **Your plants are saved** on the device, even after closing the app

## Technologies Used

- SwiftUI
- MVVM architecture
- UserDefaults (saving data)
- UserNotifications (local notifications)
